# Legal Accuracy and Safety Guardrails

## Rules
- Do not render fabricated or sample data as legal records.
- Do not claim legal conclusion without source verification.
- Any AI-generated tax guidance must cite source documents.
- Distinguish between draft analysis and authoritative legal advice.

## Review Process
- Source check before answer publication
- Human sign-off for high-impact legal or tax advice
- Clear labels for demo, draft, and verified states

## Current State
The prototype does not yet meet production legal-assurance standards, but it is intentionally transparent about this limitation.
